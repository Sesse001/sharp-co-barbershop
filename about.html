About Sharp & Co.

More than a barbershop — a craft passed down and refined.

How We Started

Sharp & Co. opened its doors on Bree Street in 2018 with one simple idea: bring back the barbershop as a place where men could get a proper cut, a straight-razor shave, and honest conversation.

Six years later, we're still doing exactly that — just with a bigger team, sharper tools, and a loyal client base that keeps coming back. We believe a haircut should be more than a transaction. It should be an experience you look forward to.

Every barber at Sharp & Co. was trained in the classics — clippers, scissors, straight razor — before earning their chair. That's our standard. No shortcuts.

What We Stand For

Three things we don't compromise on.

Craft First

We treat barbering as a craft, not a job. Every cut gets the same attention whether you're here for a trim or a full transformation.

Respect for Time

You booked a slot. That slot is yours. No double-bookings, no waiting an hour for a 20-minute cut.

Real Conversation

No awkward silence. No pushy upselling. Just good barbers, good music, and good company.
