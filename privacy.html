Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Sharp & Co. ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or book our services, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Who We Are

Sharp & Co. is a barbershop located at 123 Bree Street, Cape Town, 8001, South Africa.

Contact: hello@sharpandco.co.za · +27 21 123 4567

2. Information We Collect

When you use our website or make a booking, we may collect:

  • Booking information: your name, email address, phone number, selected service, barber, date, time, and any notes you provide
  • Contact information: your name, email, and message when you use our contact form
  • Technical information: your browser type, device type, and IP address (collected automatically by most websites)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Confirm and manage your bookings
  • Send appointment reminders or updates
  • Respond to your questions and requests
  • Improve our services and website
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How We Store Your Information

Booking information is stored securely on Google Firebase (Firestore), which uses industry-standard encryption and access controls. We retain booking records for up to 12 months for business and legal purposes, after which they are deleted.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it:

  • With our barbers, so they know who they're cutting and when
  • With service providers who help us run the website (such as Google Firebase for booking storage)
  • When required by law, court order, or regulatory authority

6. Calendar Integration

When you choose to add your booking to Google Calendar or download a calendar file, the relevant booking details are passed to your chosen calendar application. We do not control how Google Calendar or Apple Calendar handles that data — please refer to their own privacy policies.

7. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website uses minimal cookies and browser storage. We use session storage to remember if you've already seen our promotional popup, so it doesn't appear repeatedly during your visit. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

8. Your Rights

Under South African law (POPIA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to processing of your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@sharpandco.co.za.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with an updated date.
